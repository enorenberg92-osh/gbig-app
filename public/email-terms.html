Email use terms

Updated October 16, 2026. These terms cover the Gmail sending feature for authorized Green Bay Indoor Golf and Appleton Indoor Golf staff.

Authorized venue communications

Use this feature only for venue communications you are authorized to send. Connect only a business mailbox you are authorized to connect. Google authorization does not give you an app administrator role or permission to use another sender.

Review before sending

You are responsible for reviewing your selected sender, recipients, subject, and message before sending. Use appropriate recipient permission and applicable email requirements. Do not use the feature for spam, deceptive messages, or unauthorized disclosure of personal information.

Connections and delivery

Connecting Gmail does not send an email or activate the sender. Sending also requires app permissions, owner activation, a valid Google connection, and available Gmail service and quota. Google or a Workspace administrator may restrict or revoke access.

Sending cannot be assumed successful until its result is checked. A Gmail API acceptance result does not guarantee inbox delivery. If an attempt has an uncertain result, review the sender’s Sent mail before approving any replacement. The app does not automatically retry uncertain Gmail sends.

Privacy and support

The Gmail integration privacy notice explains the information used and stored. For help or removal requests, contact the app owner at enorenberg92@gmail.com.
