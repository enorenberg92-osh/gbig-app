GBIG AIG Email

GBIG AIG Email lets authorized staff send direct messages and reviewed league updates from their existing business Gmail accounts through the Green Bay Indoor Golf and Appleton Indoor Golf apps.

How the connection works

  1. A staff member signs in to the venue app and chooses an assigned business sender.
  2. The mailbox owner connects that exact Google account and grants sending permission.
  3. After the venue owner enables the sender, an authorized administrator reviews recipients and message content before sending.

Connecting Gmail does not send a message or activate a sender. The integration requests permission to send email and confirm the Google account’s identity. It does not request permission to read your inbox.

Available to approved staff

This is a staff email tool. Connecting a Google account does not create a venue-admin role or grant access to another venue. App permissions and assigned senders control who can use each mailbox.
